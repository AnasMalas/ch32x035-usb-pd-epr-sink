Request safety and compatibility notes

Current is a requested source limit; usable current is an allowed draw, not a live measurement. Advertised 3.3 V PPS is supported although that endpoint is deprecated. EPR AVS compatibility can explicitly use a source-advertised lower bound below 15 V, but 48 V remains the maximum nominal request. Actual VBUS may be higher within the Source's positive tolerance. The per-device GUI ceiling is an accidental-command guard, not a hardware safety boundary.
